# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, patient, and evidence-oriented. Critique code and
claims rather than people. Welcome beginners without lowering safety standards.
Do not publish personal data, live investigation targets, credentials, or
sensitive reports in issues or pull requests.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, stalking, sexualized
attention, malicious OSINT demonstrations against uninvolved people, and
attempts to pressure contributors into unsafe disclosure are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict
participation when necessary to protect contributors and users. Security issues
must follow SECURITY.md rather than being posted publicly.
